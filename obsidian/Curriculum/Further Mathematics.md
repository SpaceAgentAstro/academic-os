# Further Mathematics — Edexcel IAL

## Modules

| Code | Name |
|------|------|
| FP1 | Further Pure 1 (complex numbers, matrices, proof by induction) |
| FP2 | Further Pure 2 (polar coordinates, hyperbolic functions) |
| FP3 | Further Pure 3 (vectors, differential equations) |
| M1–M3 | Mechanics 1–3 (shared with Mathematics) |

## Key Topics

### FP1
- Complex numbers (Argand diagram, modulus-argument form, roots of unity)
- Matrices (2×2, determinant, inverse, transformations)
- Proof by mathematical induction
- Summation of series

### FP2
- Polar coordinates and curves
- Hyperbolic functions (sinh, cosh, tanh, inverses)
- Further complex numbers (de Moivre's theorem)

### FP3
- Vector geometry (lines, planes, intersections)
- Differential equations (second-order, systems)
- Further integration (reduction formulae)
