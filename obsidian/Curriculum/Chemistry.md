## Key Topics
- Energetics (enthalpy, Hess's law, bond energies, lattice enthalpy)
- Equilibrium (Kc, Kp, Le Chatelier's principle)
- Acid-base chemistry (pH, buffers, titrations)
- Electrochemistry (redox, electrolysis, electrode potentials)
- Kinetics (orders, activation energy, Arrhenius)
- Organic chemistry (mechanisms, functional groups)
- Spectroscopy (NMR, IR, mass spectrometry)
