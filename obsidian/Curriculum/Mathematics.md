## Key Topics by Module

### P1
- Algebra (quadratics, polynomials, inequalities)
- Coordinate geometry (circles, lines)
- Calculus (differentiation, integration)
- Exponentials and logarithms
- Binomial theorem

### P2
- Trigonometry (identities, equations)
- Sequences and series
- Proof by contradiction
- Radians
- Partial fractions

### P3
- Further calculus (integration by parts, substitution)
- Complex numbers
- Further trigonometry (double angle formulae)
- Vectors

### P4
- Differential equations
- Polar coordinates
- Maclaurin series

### S1
- Probability (conditional, Bayes)
- Discrete distributions (binomial, Poisson)
- Normal distribution
- Hypothesis testing
