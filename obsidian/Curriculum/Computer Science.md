# Computer Science — Cambridge AS & A Level (9608)

## 12 Domains

1. **Data Representation** — number systems, character encoding, images
2. **Communication and Internet Technologies** — TCP/IP, OSI model, protocols
3. **Hardware** — processors, memory, storage, I/O
4. **Processor Fundamentals** — instruction set, assembly, fetch-decode-execute
5. **System Software** — operating systems, translation
6. **Security, Privacy, and Data Integrity** — encryption, hashing
7. **Ethics and Ownership** — legislation, professional issues
8. **Databases and Bioinformatics** — relational, SQL, normalisation
9. **Algorithm Design** — sorting, searching, complexity
10. **Data Types and Structures** — linked lists, trees, stacks, queues
11. **Programming** — OOP, recursion, functional
12. **Software Development** — SDLC, testing, project management

## Key Concepts
- Big-O notation (O(1), O(n), O(n log n), O(n²))
- Boolean algebra and logic gates
- Recursive algorithms
- Entity-relationship diagrams
